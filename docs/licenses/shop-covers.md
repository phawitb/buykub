# Default shop-cover photographs

Ten nature photographs were downloaded from individual Unsplash photo pages on 2026-11-12. Each source page explicitly linked the free Unsplash License; no Unsplash+ images are included.

License: https://unsplash.com/license

The license permits downloading, modifying and using these photographs for commercial projects. These assets are used as storefront decoration, not resold as stock photos or offered as a competing image library. Attribution is optional under the license; individual photographers, source pages and retrieval details are preserved in `public/images/shop-covers/credits.json`.

The files are cropped to a wide cover ratio and optimized as WebP at 1600 × 500 and 800 × 250. Seller-uploaded covers take precedence. Fallback assignment is stable per shop; the seller can shuffle and save a different default in the appearance editor.

Google Images was the requested visual reference. Rights were verified against the originating Unsplash pages, not inferred from Google search availability.
